About Warranty Vault

Warranty Vault was created to solve a simple problem: people lose receipts and forget warranty expiry dates. Our mission is to help every household and business protect their purchases with simple, secure warranty tracking.

How does Warranty Vault store my data?

Your receipts and documents are stored securely with industry‑standard encryption.

Will I get reminders before a warranty expires?

Yes — Warranty Vault notifies you ahead of expiry so you have time to act.

Can I access Warranty Vault from multiple devices?

Yes — Warranty Vault works on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
