Delete Your Account

If you would like to permanently delete your Warranty Vault account, you can request deletion using the steps below.

How to request account deletion

Email our support team at:

support@warrantyvaultforyou.com

Include the subject line:

“Delete my account”

We will verify your identity and permanently delete your account.

What will be deleted

  • Your account (name, email, username, avatar)
  • All receipts and uploaded images
  • All warranties, notes, vaults, checklists
  • All social posts, comments, messages
  • All support messages
  • All login sessions and device IDs
  • All notifications
  • All company/team data
  • All purchase history

Retention

No data is kept after deletion. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
